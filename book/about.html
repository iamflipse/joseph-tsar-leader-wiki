About this wiki

This wiki distills one video: Joseph Tsar (@joseph_tsar_), How To Communicate Like A Leader [Full Training], posted on X on 2026-11-11 (original post). Running time is about 25.5 minutes. Tsar runs the Elite Speak communication coaching program and says he has coached over 100 executives and entrepreneurs.

What is here and what is not

  • Here: plain-language summaries of each idea, how to apply it, and short quotes (each under about 30 words) with approximate [mm:ss] times.
  • Not here: the full transcript. This is Joseph Tsar's content; watch the original for the complete training, visuals (the Articulate Ascent chart, the symptom list, the emotion wheel) and his delivery.
  • Left out on purpose: the promo segments (a free diagnosis-call pitch for his 90-day Elite Speak program with a short client testimonial around 16:40, and an email opt-in for his own pre-speaking routine at the end). The Pre-Speaking Routine page here is assembled only from what he says in the video, not from that download.

How it was made

  • Transcribed locally with Whisper (large-v3-turbo). The first pass had broken timestamps (a repeated passage and a jump from 05:29 to 09:28), so the audio was re-transcribed in fixed 20-second windows. Quote times are accurate to roughly ±10 seconds.
  • A few visual-only or unexplained items are flagged on the relevant pages as "not covered in the video".
