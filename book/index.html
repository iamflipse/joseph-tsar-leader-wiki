Communicate Like a Leader

The one-line thesis of the training: most people polish the surface of their speech (filler words, frameworks, word choice), but the "low-quality" problems they feel are symptoms of three deeper, high-quality problems: intention, focus, and body & breath. Train those like an athlete and most of the surface problems fade.

Distilled from Joseph Tsar's How To Communicate Like A Leader [Full Training] (about 25 minutes, posted on X on 2026-11-11).
